Team: Cohen ter Heide, Danelly Joseph, Riya Daiya

Course: IAT 355 - Data Visualization

Project Overview

This visualization project examines two parallel narratives:

  • UFO Sightings: Cultural phenomena shaped by media, perception, and human psychology
  • Exoplanet Discoveries: Scientific progress driven by technology, evidence, and methodology
