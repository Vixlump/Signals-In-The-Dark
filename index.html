In 1977, NASA launched the Voyager space probes 1 and 2 to study the outer solar system. On the probes, they attached this 12-inch gold plated record, intended for any intelligent extraterrestrial life form who may find it.

We’ve all heard these words a gazillion times: Extraterrestrial life. Aliens. UFOs.
