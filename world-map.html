Where the Sky Lights Up: Global UFO Sightings

This World Map of Global UFO sightings, grounds the abstract idea of UFOs in geographical reality, showing where sightings have been reported worldwide; to get the bigger picture.

2010s

Insight

Most reports come from the United States, not because UFOs prefer it, but because of reporting access, cultural influence, and technology. Still, sightings appear worldwide — in cities, deserts, and coastlines.

Why This Visualization Belongs Here

“Where?” is the first natural question when exploring UFO phenomena. This map provides spatial grounding before moving into temporal trends, establishing a foundation for understanding the data.
