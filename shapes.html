Our space missions so far

This visualization presents a detailed mission analysis, allowing you to track the activity volume and success rate of major space companies over time. Each bubble on the chart represents a company's total launches in a given year.

Mission Analysis Insights

The **Punchcard Chart** above aggregates four key dimensions:

  • **Y-Axis:** Space companies, sorted by overall mission count.
  • **X-Axis:** Mission launch year, showing temporal trends.
  • **Bubble Size:** Represents the total number of launches by that company in that year (Volume).
  • **Color:** Shows the mission **Success Rate** (Blue for high success, Red for high failure).

Hover over any bubble to see the exact breakdown of successful and failed missions for that company and year.
